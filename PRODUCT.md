# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers and recruiters evaluating Simon Hylander for senior or lead engineering roles. They arrive with limited time, often between meetings or after scanning many similar profiles. They need a fast read on caliber, scope of impact, and whether the person behind the site thinks like a builder who cares about craft.

## Product Purpose

hylander.dev is a personal portfolio that establishes credibility for senior and lead engineering opportunities. Success is not measured by form submissions alone: a visitor should leave convinced of depth, judgment, and taste, even if they do not contact Simon immediately. The site demonstrates technical leadership through the quality of the interface itself, curated proof of work (experience, projects, brag doc), and a voice that feels human rather than templated.

## Brand Personality

Playful · Builder · Curious

Warm technical confidence without corporate stiffness. The tone invites exploration (projects, stack, brag doc) while signaling that UX and productive tooling are first-class concerns, not afterthoughts. Personality shows in neo-brutalist structure and deliberate details, not in gimmicks or novelty for its own sake.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS portfolio templates: hero metrics, identical icon-card grids, gradient text, glass cards as decoration.
- Dark-mode neon "hacker" developer aesthetic.
- Obvious AI-generated UI slop: interchangeable purple gradients, meaningless glassmorphism, side-stripe accent borders, modal-first interactions.
- Corporate resume PDF pasted onto a webpage: stock icon rows, flat grids, no point of view.

## Design Principles

1. **The UI is the proof.** The portfolio should feel designed by someone who ships exceptional experiences; polish and intentionality are part of the hiring signal.
2. **Credibility before conversion.** Optimize for a convincing read on senior/lead caliber; contact is a natural follow-on, not the only win.
3. **Personality without gimmicks.** Playful and curious energy comes from structure, copy, and craft choices, not from novelty widgets or trend-chasing effects.
4. **Neo-brutalist, ownable.** Offset shadows, clear borders, and bold type are a deliberate lane; push the existing direction further rather than drifting toward template brutalism or SaaS polish.
5. **Practice what you preach.** References to UX obsession and productive tooling must be visible in navigation clarity, readable hierarchy, and respectful motion, not only in bio copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Pragmatic baseline: semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible navigation and interactive elements, visible focus states, alt text on meaningful imagery, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for Framer Motion and CSS transitions. No formal WCAG audit target yet; revisit contrast on the purple/lavender palette as the visual system evolves.
