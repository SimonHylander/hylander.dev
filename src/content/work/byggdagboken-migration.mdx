## Decisions

- **Strangler fig.** The new Next.js client takes over the legacy system route by route.
- **Route-level canaries.** CloudFront Functions roll each migrated route out gradually.
- **A backend-for-frontend.** The native apps read through a BFF layer that aggregates their data.
- **One identity.** OAuth2 and OpenID Connect with PKCE unify sign-in across platforms.
