## Decisions

Uppgraderingspaket is an AI price matching service for PC hardware, built as a RAG pipeline in three steps:

1. **Scrape.** An Inngest job scrapes product listings.
2. **Embed.** The products go into a vector database as embeddings.
3. **Answer.** Chat search retrieves the matching products and feeds them to an LLM as context.

It runs on Next.js and Vercel.
